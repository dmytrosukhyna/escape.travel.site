# App Store Connect — listing copy

Every field App Store Connect asks for, in Russian, Ukrainian and English.
Character limits are Apple's and the counts are what the text below actually
uses.

Nothing here claims a user count, a rating, an award, a press mention or a
partnership, because none of those exist yet. If any of that changes, it can be
added — but it has to be true on the day the listing goes live, and provable.

**Description** (4000):

> **One request instead of fifteen tabs.**
>
> One trip means flights, a hotel, a route, the weather, car hire, restaurants
> and connections — each in its own tab. You hold it together by hand, and no
> tab knows what your whole day looks like.
>
> Escape! is an AI agent, not a search box. You say where and for how long. It
> chooses, connects the decisions to each other, and hands back a finished trip.
>
> **THE NEW YEAR TRIP**
> Say into the snow, into the warm or into the city, and the agent picks the
> destination, the flight out on the 30th, a place with a view, a table for New
> Year's Eve and a plan for every day of the holiday.
>
> **WHAT THE AGENT DOES**
> • Assembles the whole trip: the flight, the stay, the route, the food, the
>   time on the road
> • Explains every choice — why the morning flight, why that hotel
> • Keeps to the budget you named and shows what it is made of
> • Rebuilds the plan when the day changes: a delay, rain, a new gate
> • Turns eight hours on a layover into a day in the city and gets you back in
>   time
> • Writes to you first when a destination you saved comes down to your budget
>
> **TOGETHER**
> If there is nobody to go with, Escape! matches travellers on dates, route,
> budget, pace and interests — not on who you already know.
>
> **PLAINLY, THE LIMITS**
> Escape! is not a carrier, a hotel or a tour operator. Bookings happen with the
> provider and their terms govern the transaction. The agent can be wrong: check
> visas, documents, connection times and cancellation terms yourself.
>
> Some capabilities describe a product in development. What works today and what
> comes later is set out at escapetravel.site.
